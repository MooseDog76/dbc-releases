# Security

**Found a security problem?** Please tell me privately: use **"Report a vulnerability"** on this repository's Security tab.
I'll reply within 14 days.

- Only the newest version gets fixes. Get it from this page's **Releases** - DBC doesn't update itself yet.
- I'll give at least 12 months' notice before updates stop. If they stop, the app keeps working and your recipes stay yours.
- If I learn a security hole is being used, I'll fix it, release, and tell users the same week.
- **If there's no release and no reply for 12 months, treat the app as unsupported.** To remove it: on your Android phone,
  press and hold **DBC**, then tap **Uninstall** (or Settings > Apps > DBC > Uninstall). DBC keeps your recipes, photos,
  shopping list and settings only inside the app on your phone, so they are deleted with it - DBC can't export the recipe
  book yet, so keep anything you need (your own photos, the book's page numbers) before uninstalling. Your Claude key is
  kept in the phone's locked store and is deleted with the app too.
- The app is built following a written set of checks (the Safety Net).
